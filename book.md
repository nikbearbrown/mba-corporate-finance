# Corporate Finance with AI

**Working title:** Corporate Finance with AI
**Author:** Nik Bear Brown
**Sibling project to:** Computational Finance with AI (Book 1)

## Audience

MBA finance students who have completed an investments core (or Book 1: Computational Finance with AI). They know what stocks and bonds are, can run a DCF on a public company, and can use Claude with the three-beat method (verify → calculate → sanity-check). They have not yet sat in a CFO's seat. This book takes them there.

A reader coming directly from Book 1 should pick up Book 2 with the toolkit installed and the verification habits formed. A reader starting fresh with Book 2 should still be able to follow — Chapter 1 carries the onboarding load for both audiences.

## Scope

Standard MBA Corporate Finance core: how a CFO reads the firm from inside, allocates capital across investments and financing structures, distributes capital to shareholders, and executes the strategic transactions that define the role. Investments, derivatives pricing, and quantitative trading are out of scope (those live in Book 1 or its successors).

The book is organized as a 15-chapter three-act arc:

- **Act 1 (Ch 1-3) — Read the Firm.** Inside-the-firm perspective; financial statements, working capital, the operational reality CFOs see daily.
- **Act 2 (Ch 4-8) — Allocate Capital.** Capital budgeting, cost of capital, risk-adjusted rates and real options, then the paired Ch 7-8 pivot on capital structure (MM theory + real-world frictions).
- **Act 3 (Ch 9-15) — Distribute and Execute.** Payout, security issuance, M&A, risk management, international, behavioral, capstone synthesis.

## Voice

Feynman-flavored as defined in root `CLAUDE.md` §6. Conversational authority, specification before argument, math visible, first person sparingly. Maya — a senior analyst in the CFO's office at a mid-cap industrial firm — is the running protagonist who carries the inside-the-firm perspective across chapters.

This book has no per-book voice samples in `style/` yet. Drafts produced before that folder is populated should be marked `voice-unanchored` and reviewed with that limitation in mind. The first chapter of this book is a voice-setting exercise.

## Prerequisites

- **Inherited from Book 1 if read:** the three-beat method, the Claude verification toolkit, comfort with DCF mechanics from outside the firm.
- **Novel for Book 2:** firm-level cash flow projection (Ch 4), theoretical framework comfort with idealizations like MM (Ch 7), currency mechanics (Ch 13), behavioral economics primer (Ch 14).

## Distinguishing claim

Standard MBA corporate finance textbooks teach the topics. This book teaches the **integrated CFO recommendation** — investment, financing, and payout decisions held simultaneously — with AI verification discipline embedded throughout. At no point does the book retreat into Excel-based pedagogy.
