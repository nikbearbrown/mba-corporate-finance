# Corporate Finance with AI
## Compact Tik TOC Architecture

**Book type:** MBA finance textbook  
**Status:** Retrospective architecture for developed content  

## Core Thesis

This book teaches corporate finance as decision-making under capital constraints: valuation, investment, financing, risk, payout, M&A, and governance, with AI used to support analysis and communication.

## Primary Reader

MBA students, managers, founders, and analysts who need to make or evaluate corporate finance decisions.

## Learning Arc

1. **Value foundations:** time value, cash flows, risk, discount rates, and valuation.
2. **Corporate decisions:** capital budgeting, capital structure, payout, working capital, and real options.
3. **Strategic finance:** M&A, international finance, governance, reporting, and integrated recommendations.

## Chapter Architecture

Each chapter should start with a CFO decision, build the financial logic, run the analysis, and end with a recommendation memo.

## Terminal Capability

The reader can evaluate corporate finance choices and communicate a defensible recommendation with assumptions and sensitivities.

## Adoption Risks

- AI can obscure spreadsheet/model errors.
- Current market examples age.
- Students may memorize formulas without decision logic.
